About Caretaker

A full-service partnerin security & risk.

Caretaker Security Service Company provides tailor-made security and risk management solutions to a wide range of clientele throughout Uganda. Originally established and incorporated in Kampala under the Companies Act (Section 16:1), the company has since expanded its resources across the country — with special emphasis on specialised security and risk management, including international and domestic site security.

At Caretaker Security, we are not just a security company — we are your trusted guardians, dedicated to safeguarding what matters to you.

Our vision

“To protect, secure and inspire trust for unwavering caretakers of your safety.”

Mission statement

To provide up-to-date, timely and comprehensive international and domestic site security solutions in Uganda, while constantly adapting to changing industry trends.

Quality statement

To Caretaker Security there is only one level of quality and service — and that is to exceed our clients’ expectations. Entirely customer focused, we listen to our customers’ needs and deliver expert security solutions through highly trained, motivated and experienced management and personnel.
