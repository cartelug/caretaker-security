Privacy Policy

How this sitetreats your information.

Caretaker Security Services Limited ("Caretaker", "we", "us") publishes this website to describe our services and make it easy to reach us. This policy explains what happens to information you share with it. Last updated 21 September 2026.

What information is collected

This website is a set of static pages with no server or database behind it. It does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not set cookies. The only information we ever receive from a visitor is what that visitor chooses to give us directly — by phone, by email, or through the enquiry form on our Contact page.

The enquiry form composes an email in your own email application when you submit it. Whatever you type — your name, phone or email, the service you're asking about, and your message — is sent from your device to ours the same way any email is; nothing is transmitted to or stored on this website itself, because there is nothing on the website capable of storing it.

Why it's collected

Solely to respond to your enquiry — to understand what you need, get back to you, and, where relevant, arrange or quote for a security assignment.

How enquiries are handled

Enquiries reach us at our published phone numbers and email addresses, where they're reviewed by our operations team and responded to directly. We don't use automated systems to screen or respond to enquiries.

Whether information is shared with third parties

No. We do not sell, rent or share the information in your enquiry with third parties. It is used internally, only to respond to you.

How long information is retained

An enquiry lives in the inbox of the person who received it for as long as is reasonable to service your request and keep a normal business record of it — there is no fixed automatic deletion schedule. If you'd like us to delete information you've sent us, write to ctaker10@gmail.com and we will action it.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use cookies, and does not run any analytics, advertising or social-media tracking scripts. Nothing about your visit is recorded anywhere by us.

Questions about this policy

Write to ctaker10@gmail.com or use the details on our Contact page.
